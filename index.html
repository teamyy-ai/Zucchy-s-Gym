Gym Tricks

Train smarter. Grow stronger.

Simple habits that make every session count, from your very first warm-up to your next personal record.

Tips that actually work

01Training

Warm up before you lift

Spend 5–10 minutes on light cardio, then a few easy sets of your first exercise. Warm muscles move better and are less likely to get hurt.

02Training

Add a little, every week

Muscles grow when the work gets harder. Add a rep, a little weight or an extra set week by week. That’s progressive overload.

03Form

Form before weight

Master each movement with lighter loads first. Clean reps build more muscle and keep your joints safe.

04Training

Big lifts first

Do squats, deadlifts, presses and rows early, while you’re fresh. Finish with smaller exercises like curls and raises.

05Form

Brace and breathe

Before a heavy rep, breathe deep into your belly and tighten your core. Breathe out once you’re past the hardest part.

06Form

Use the full range

Lower the weight under control and move through the whole range of motion. Half reps give you half the results.

07Nutrition

Eat enough protein

Include protein at every meal: eggs, fish, chicken, beans or dairy. Many lifters aim for about 1.6 g per kg of body weight a day.

08Nutrition

Drink water all day

Even mild dehydration makes training feel harder. Bring a bottle and sip between sets.

09Recovery

Sleep is training too

Most adults need 7–9 hours a night. Your muscles repair and grow while you rest, not while you lift.

10Recovery

Plan your rest days

Give each muscle group about 48 hours before training it hard again, and take at least one full rest day a week.

11Mindset

Track every workout

Write down your weights, sets and reps, or let Zucchy AI do it for you. Seeing progress keeps you going.

12Mindset

Consistency beats intensity

Three solid sessions every week for a year beat one heroic week. Show up, even on low-energy days.

Daily motivation

The hardest lift is getting out the door. You’ve already done that.

Good gym manners

A great gym is one where everyone feels welcome.

  • Re-rack your weights when you’re done
  • Wipe down benches and machines after use
  • Share stations and let others work in between sets
  • Keep phone calls off the training floor
  • Never film other members without asking
  • Ask for a spot on heavy lifts. We’re happy to help

New to training, or managing an injury or health condition? Check with your doctor first, and ask any of our instructors to show you the ropes.
